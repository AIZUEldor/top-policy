Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

TOP Platform is a job search and worker finding platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Profile image
  • CV or resume file
  • Location information
  • Push notification token

How We Use Information

We use this information to create user profiles, connect job seekers with employers, show nearby jobs, allow CV upload, send notifications, and improve the platform.

Account Registration

Users currently register using a phone number.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Some profile and job-related information may be visible to other users when needed for job search, job applications, or employer-worker communication.

Location Data

Location data is used to show nearby jobs and improve job matching. Users can control location permission from their device settings.

Push Notifications

We use push notification tokens to send important updates such as job application updates and platform notifications.

Account Deletion

Users can request account deletion from inside the app through Profile → Delete Account.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact TOP Platform support.
