ALP Jobs Child Safety Standards

Our commitment to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation.

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

ALP Jobs has a strict zero tolerance policy against child sexual abuse material, child sexual exploitation, grooming, trafficking, harassment, or any content or behavior that endangers children.

2. Prohibited Content and Behavior

The following are strictly prohibited on ALP Jobs:

  • Any sexual content involving minors.
  • Attempts to exploit, groom, threaten, or harm children.
  • Sharing, requesting, or distributing child sexual abuse material.
  • Using job posts, profiles, messages, or contact details for unsafe or illegal activity involving minors.
  • Any behavior that violates applicable child safety laws or Google Play policies.

3. Reporting and Enforcement

Users can report suspicious, harmful, or abusive activity related to child safety by contacting us directly. We review reports and may remove content, restrict accounts, suspend users, or take other enforcement actions.

4. Cooperation with Authorities

If we identify content or behavior involving child sexual abuse or exploitation, we may report it to the appropriate authorities and cooperate with law enforcement when required by applicable law.

5. User Safety

ALP Jobs is a job marketplace platform. We encourage users to communicate professionally and responsibly. Employers and job seekers must not use the platform for any illegal, abusive, exploitative, or unsafe activity.

6. Contact for Child Safety Concerns

If you need to report a child safety concern related to ALP Jobs, please contact us:

Email: aizuegroup@gmail.com

7. Updates

We may update these Child Safety Standards from time to time to comply with legal requirements, platform policies, and safety best practices.
