Delete Account Request

TOP Platform allows users to delete their account directly from the mobile application.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open TOP Platform.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Select "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm the deletion request.

What Happens After Deletion

  • Your account becomes inaccessible.
  • Your profile will no longer be visible to other users.
  • Your login access will be removed.
  • Some records may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or audit purposes.

Need Help?

If you experience problems deleting your account, contact TOP Platform support.
